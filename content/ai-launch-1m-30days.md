## 7. The actual day-to-day advice and lessons

### Outreach
- For beginners: go for **quantity** of outreach to land any client.
- Once you have case studies: go for **quality** to land better clients.
- The best outreach gives prospects **tangible, usable assets** they can plug and play right now.
- If you can do **both quantity and quality**, your outreach stands out massively.

### Funnel / conversion
- Paid events produce higher-intent leads than free events, but they can still be broke.
- He thought a paid ticket would filter out broke people — **it didn't**. Many paid $97 but couldn't afford the main offer.
- **Add friction before the call** (application with a "this is a four-figure investment" checkbox) to filter broke leads.
- **Order of funnel pages matters**:
  - Putting the price page before opt-in increased conversion from opt-in to ticket but dropped total opt-ins.
  - Putting the VIP upsell before the survey increased VIP take rate but hurt survey responses.
  - He kept the VIP page before survey because OTO revenue was more important.
- **Reducing friction at opt-in** (direct popup capture) increased opt-ins but dropped GA conversion from 35% to 16%.

### Ads
- Re-invest ticket revenue into ads as fast as possible. The launch is essentially **self-liquidating**.
- At the end of the campaign, stop worrying about ROAS and spend aggressively to buy every lead you can before the event.
- He references Jeremy Haynes: *"The guy spending $100K at 1.5x ROAS makes way more than the guy spending $20K at 4x ROAS."*
- The highest-performing ad was a **straight income call-out** ("if you're making $10–15K/mo").
- The second-highest was a **video ad educating a framework + stitched CTA**.
- They also ran the client's top-performing organic reels as ads with a CTA stitched at the end.
- **Retargeting flow**:
  - Retarget non-attendees to watch replay.
  - WhatsApp + email sequences to push replay.
  - If they watch 30+ min and don't apply, retarget with urgency / offer ads.

### Email / list reactivation
- Flash sales work best when tied to a **rationale / special event** (he made one up about "OPEX").
- Email only the people who opted in but didn't buy — they are semi-warm.
- Build anticipation a day before the sale to spike the next day's revenue.
- Leave the coupon "accidentally" valid after the official deadline to capture extra buyers.

### Webinar / delivery
- **Hype up the final pitch day** with Q&A / extra-long session to keep retention high.
- Day 4 is pitch day — he noticed a drop-off and told attendees it would be extra long with Q&A, which brought retention back up.
- **The client butchered the pitch** on day 4, which hurt booked-call numbers.
- Day 5 the client redeemed himself with a proper pitch and got more booked calls.

### Hiring / team
- Initially he wanted a zero-employee agency run by AI.
- By the end he hired a **full-time AI operator** and brought back a **full-time copywriter**.
- His assistant **Rocco** is 16, skipped school, stayed up all night, and became his right hand.
- He became picky with hiring because he doesn't want to train someone who leaves.
- He values **moldable, hungry operators** over polished but inflexible veterans.

### Mindset
- He warns about **greed and ego** creeping in once you make money.
- Took a lower-margin deal to build a relationship and case study; it opened the door to bigger future launches.
- Client appreciation and reaction are his biggest motivation.
- "Money doesn't sleep" — brutal work hours are part of the game.

## 11. What he did vs. what he says AI did

- **90% of the webinar build was AI**, according to the client and Yahya.
- AI built: landing pages, upsell pages, workbooks, ad scripts, slides, ad creative, email/SMS sequences, funnel logic, lead research.
- Human still required for: high-level strategy, client calls, ad account management, crisis handling, hiring decisions, final pitch delivery by the client.
- By the end he admits **he was still doing too much copy work himself** and that the workflow wasn't as efficient as he hoped, so he hires copy + operator help.

## 12. Final takeaways

- **The model is real and replicable**, but it's not "set and forget." It requires intense execution, ad management, and crisis handling.
- **AI removes the labor bottleneck** for copy, design, and funnel building, but **strategy, client management, and paid-media judgment are still human-driven.**
- **Paid challenge funnels are a cash-flow machine** if you reinvest fast and don't sit on cash.
- **Lead quality is more important than lead quantity.** A paid ticket does not guarantee ability to buy the back-end.
- **The biggest ROI came from fast iteration**: testing funnel page order, opt-in flow, survey placement, upsell timing, flash-sale rationale, and ad creative.
- **Free events may scale bigger** for some markets than paid events.
- **Hiring the right people and letting go of "one-man show" ego is the next level.**
